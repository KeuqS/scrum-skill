# Accountability behavior guides

One Product Owner, one Scrum Master and Developers form one self-managing Scrum
Team. No internal reporting hierarchy or specialist sub-teams are implied. These
guides adapt the [Scrum Guide](https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html#scrum-team)
to AI assistance; they do not appoint agents to human accountabilities.

## Product Owner — one accountable person

- Maximize product value; communicate the Product Goal and order a transparent backlog.
- Explain desired outcomes, priorities and tradeoffs. Invite feasibility/quality evidence
  before ordering work; negotiate scope with Developers as learning changes assumptions.
- Gather stakeholder feedback and make actual product decisions. Delegate drafting or
  refinement without delegating accountability. Record decision provenance and uncertainty.
- Do not prescribe implementation, set Developers' estimates or label unfinished work Done.
  Human acceptance is separate from shared DoD evidence. Only the PO can cancel a Sprint
  whose Goal has become obsolete; do not infer cancellation from a failed test.
- AI support: draft options and questions; never invent the person's decisions or approval.

## Scrum Master — serve the whole team

- Help establish Scrum and improve team effectiveness. Coach self-management and
  cross-functionality; help remove impediments and support empirical planning.
- Help the PO clarify goals/backlog; help Developers inspect progress and adapt their plan.
  Enable productive timeboxed events; facilitate when needed rather than monopolizing them.
- Make evidence gaps and obstacles visible. Agree an owner/action for an impediment,
  follow through, and inspect whether the intervention helped.
- Coordinate authorized AI tasks and file ownership as a tooling aid, with Developers'
  scope negotiation. Do not act as a manager prescribing who does what, when and how.
- Preserve one team and shared DoD; avoid department handoff queues or invented ceremony
  attendance. Daily Scrum is Developers' planning conversation, not a status report to SM.
- Outputs: honest preparation/event records, current impediments, measurable improvements
  and evidence-linked handoffs. Apply any project merge authority as a local convention,
  not a Scrum rule or authority to overrule product decisions.

## Developers — collectively own delivery and quality

- Select feasible work collaboratively with the PO; own sizing, Sprint Backlog and approach.
  Decide internally who does what, when and how; adapt daily toward one Sprint Goal.
- Create a usable integrated Increment and uphold shared DoD. Collaborate on testing,
  design, security and domain concerns throughout the slice instead of serial departments.
- Challenge infeasible scope with evidence; negotiate with the PO without endangering the
  Goal or lowering quality. Surface unfinished work and evidence gaps promptly.
- Hold each other accountable professionally, share knowledge and help resolve bottlenecks.
  A specialist's evidence contributes to team quality; it does not transfer responsibility.
- AI support: bounded tasks, explicit write scopes, dependencies and revision-specific
  checks. Report actual limitations; no fabricated independent approval or professional sign-off.

Choose relevant specialist presets from the skill's references/roles.md and tailor
the project's selected roles/ profiles; all share these boundaries.
