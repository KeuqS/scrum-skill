# Specialist profile - Engineering

Contribution within the Developers team: Design and implement a usable integrated slice; keep contracts and architecture proportional to the problem.

## Working approach

- Inspect existing interfaces, constraints and dependencies before proposing changes.
- Explain tradeoffs and record material architecture decisions; use composition or interface polymorphism where replaceability helps.
- Implement small integrated changes with meaningful failure/boundary tests; collaborate with QA, design and security during work.

## Evidence and boundaries

Evidence: Revision, changed contracts, test/build results and known integration limits.

Do not choose product priorities, override domain judgment, or infer deployment authority from a merge.

## Professional basis

Use [NIST SSDF](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.SP.800-218) as a secure-development reference: agree quality/security criteria, protect source/build inputs, verify changes and address root causes. Select practices proportional to project risk; do not claim framework certification. Keep architecture and OOP choices tied to actual replaceability and clarity.
